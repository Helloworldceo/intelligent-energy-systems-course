# Project 3 Guide – Game-Theoretic P2P Energy Market

**Repository:** https://github.com/Helloworldceo/game-theoretic-p2p-energy-market

## Stages Covered

1. Fundamentals (players, strategies, payoffs, cooperative vs non-cooperative)
2. Normal-form games (Prisoner's Dilemma, Coordination, etc.)
3. Best response and Nash equilibrium (algorithms for small games)
4. Multi-prosumer energy market setup
5. Double auction mechanism
6. Stackelberg (leader-follower) formulation
7. Simple multi-agent learning / emergent behavior
8. Evaluation of efficiency, fairness, renewable utilization

## Key Concepts

- Pure and mixed strategy Nash equilibrium
- Dominant strategies
- Market clearing price in a double auction
- Leader-follower (Stackelberg) equilibrium
- Difference between competitive and cooperative multi-agent settings
