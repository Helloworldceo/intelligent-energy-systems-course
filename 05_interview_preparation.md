# Interview / Conceptual Checklist

Be ready to explain (with a simple example):

**Control**
- Difference between open-loop and closed-loop
- What steady-state error is and why integral action removes it
- What derivative action does
- Meaning of rise time, settling time, overshoot
- Why anti-windup is needed

**Reinforcement Learning**
- Bellman equation (intuition)
- Exploration vs exploitation
- Why we need a target network in DQN
- What experience replay does
- Difference between value-based and policy-based methods
- Advantage of PPO over vanilla policy gradient

**Game Theory**
- Nash equilibrium definition
- Dominant strategy vs Nash
- Prisoner's Dilemma and why mutual defection is the equilibrium
- What a double auction is
- Stackelberg vs simultaneous-move games

**Energy Systems**
- Why battery SOC constraints matter
- Self-consumption vs grid feed-in
- Peak shaving
- How control + RL + markets can work together in a microgrid
